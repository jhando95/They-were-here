Neighborly Unit. Clones bleed green glitter, buffer when confronted with
contradictions, and are *incapable of being rude first*.

### GLORP PRIME (final boss)

**HP 30 · Dodge 10 · Two actions per round**

- **Turf Slam** +4, 1d8 — throws a lawn at you. The whole lawn.
- **Spore Burst** — everyone nearby: Weird check DC 12 or take 1d4 and hiccup
  glowing bubbles for a scene (disadvantage on stealth, obviously).
- **Absorb** — eats scenery (potluck table, riding mower, a gazebo) to regain 1d6 HP.
  Each absorbed object should be funnier than the last.

**Weaknesses:**
- **Salt: double damage.** A full margarita-rimmer to the face is 2d6.
- **The Weirdness Gambit:** each act of genuine human weirdness on the field that
  round (an ally's signature bit, a flamboyant power use, free jazz, Biscuit in
  sunglasses) makes him **buffer — lose one action**. Two weird acts = he skips
  his whole round, visibly rendering a loading bar.
- He cannot interrupt anyone who is being *sincerely polite* to him. This is
  exploitable and he hates i— no, he doesn't hate anything. It's fine.
  **IT IS FINE, FELLOW BEINGS.**
