# GM Screen — everything on one page

## Rolls
**d20 + stat vs DC.** Easy **8** · Tricky **12** · Hard **16** · Out-of-this-world **20**
Advantage/disadvantage = roll 2d20 keep best/worst. **Nat 20:** works + delight.
**Nat 1:** works-ish + the universe's joke + **regain 1 WP**.
**Failure = progress + complication.** Always. Roll the d10 Complications table when stuck.

## Economies
| Resource | Cap | Gain | Spend |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HP** | 8+Brawn | rest, casserole (1d6) | 0 HP = out of scene + roll a **Mark** (d6) |
| **WP** | 3 | nat 1 · accept complication · sleep · Duke's mystery special | Surge 1 · SUBLIME 2 (1/session) |
| **Luck** | 3/session | jokes · heart · Big Human Energy | reroll ANY die (yours or GM's, incl. ally rolls) |
| **Glow** | — | end of each act (+bonus for side-quest chains) | +1 stat (max +4) · new power (roll twice keep one) · SUBLIME unlock |

## Combat
Player acts first (unless ambushed — Weird to un-ambush). Move + one thing.
Attack: d20+Brawn (Brains gadgets/thrown, Weird powers) vs Dodge. PC Dodge = 10+Brawn.
Damage: improvised **d4** · solid **d6** · absurd-but-committed **d8**.
**SALT: double damage vs gelatinous; dissolves spore-grass.** Fleeing is always an option.
Ally on-scene: +1d6 to one roll/scene; if it fails, the *ally* eats the complication.

## The two clocks (THE VISE — full rules: villains-and-twists.md)
**👁 Suspicion (Neighborhood Watch, starts 2)** — the ALIENS noticing the PC
**+1** loud/weird in public · caught snooping · gnome destroyed
**+2** breaking in seen · party incident · night tower climb
**−1** a day of Visible Normalcy (mow, wave, bake sale) · framing the raccoons
**4:** camera-gnome on PC's lawn · **6:** all gnomes face them; GARY-2 at the window
**8:** wellness check (2 Units + clipboard + bundt cake) · **10:** 2:47 abduction attempt

**🫥 Reputation (starts 1)** — the HUMANS deciding the PC is crazy (MARSH-2's clock)
**+1** public claims w/o proof · failed public scene · Truthers endorse the PC · MARSH-2 unopposed for a session
**−1** Hard evidence shown privately · a week of normalcy · Deborah vouches · **−2** Arlene publishes (needs 2 Hard pieces)
**3:** Gazette "concerned" item · **5:** allies pressured · **7:** THE INTERVENTION · **9:** the Committal van
Evidence tiers: Rumor 0 · Trace 1 (photos, samples) · **Hard 2** (footage, documents, freed pod-guest).
Going public with <2 Hard pieces BACKFIRES (+1 Reputation).
One Opposition move per session; one fired twist per session, tops (Twist Deck: app → Scenes tab).
