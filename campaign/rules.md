# The PORCHLIGHT System
### Rules-lite suburban paranormal rules for one player + one GM

Everything runs on **one d20** and **four stats**. If a rule slows down the fun,
the fun wins.

---

## 1. Character Creation (5 minutes)

1. **Name, age, and what Pinebrook knows you for.** ("Riley — the night nurse who
   once unclogged the school fountain with a defibrillator.")
2. **Assign +3, +2, +1, +0** to the four stats:

| Stat | Use it to... |
|---|---|
| **BRAWN** | fight, sprint, climb trellises, carry a pod person to safety |
| **BRAINS** | notice, recall, repair, spot the Jell-O wobbling against the breeze |
| **CHARM** | persuade, lie, de-escalate, win over a skeptical waitress |
| **WEIRD** | use powers, sense alien activity, trust the gut, talk to the dog |

3. **Derived numbers:**
   - **HP = 8 + Brawn.** At 0 HP you're not dead — you're *out of the scene*
     (captured, concussed, hiding in a kiddie pool) and come back with a mark of it.
   - **Dodge = 10 + Brawn.** Enemies roll against this.
   - **Weird Points (WP): start with 2, max 3.** Fuel for your power.
   - **Luck: start each session with 3.**
4. **Roll (or pick) one Wacky Power** from the d20 table in `tables.md`, and decide
   with the GM how you got glorped.
5. **Gear:** whatever fits your job, plus a fanny pack, a flashlight, and one
   sentimental item the GM is absolutely allowed to make plot-relevant.

## 2. Doing Things

When the outcome is uncertain and interesting: **d20 + stat vs DC.**

| DC | Feels like |
|---|---|
| 8 | Easy — climbing the Hendersons' trellis |
| 12 | Tricky — lying to a clone about why you're in their garage |
| 16 | Hard — hotwiring a riding mower mid-chase |
| 20 | Out-of-this-world — catching the drone with a pool skimmer, blindfolded |

- **Advantage / Disadvantage:** roll 2d20, keep best/worst. Given by clever
  setup, allies, powers, or being covered in Jell-O.
- **Nat 20:** it works *and* something delightful happens.
- **Nat 1:** it works-ish, but the universe gets a joke at your expense — and you
  **regain 1 WP** as an apology.
- **Failure never means "nothing happens."** The GM gives progress with a
  complication: you get in, but Suspicion +1; you hear the intel, but Biscuit
  ate the evidence.

### Luck (3 per session)
Spend 1 to **reroll any roll** — yours or one the GM just made. The GM hands out
bonus Luck for jokes, heart, and conspicuously human behavior. Hoarding Luck
into the finale is wise; the finale is hungry.

### Help from Allies
An ally on-scene who could plausibly help adds **+1d6** to one roll per scene
(their "thing" — Wendell's gadgets, Priya's drone, Deborah's clipboard, Biscuit's
nose). If the roll fails, the *ally* suffers the complication. Protect your weirdos.

## 3. Weird Powers

- Using your power's minor, flavorful version is **free** (hover 3 inches to
  avoid gravel; taste that the radio is playing smooth jazz).
- Using it to **change a scene** costs **1 WP** and usually a **Weird roll**
  (DC set by the GM).
- Regain 1 WP when you: roll a nat 1, accept a GM-offered complication, sleep,
  or eat something questionable at Duke's Diner.

---

## 4. Fighting (keep it fast and dumb)

Combat is scene-flavored, not grid-based. Use the app map for positioning vibes.

1. **The player always acts first** unless ambushed (Weird check to un-ambush).
2. On your turn: **move + one thing** (attack, power, stunt, scheme).
3. **Attack:** d20 + Brawn (or Brains for gadgets/thrown, Weird for powers) vs the
   enemy's Dodge. Damage by weapon: improvised d4, solid d6 (bat, skillet),
   absurd-but-committed d8 (riding mower, T-ball pitching machine).
4. **Enemies attack:** d20 + their bonus vs your Dodge.
5. **Salt** deals **double damage** to anything gelatinous and dissolves
   spore-grass on contact.
6. Fleeing is always an option and frequently the plot.

At **0 HP**, the PC is out of the scene, not dead — the Collective finds violence
*gauche*. Being captured is a story opportunity (the pods are actually very
comfortable, which is its own horror).

---

## 5. Suspicion (the town-wide clock)

A 0–10 track in the app, starting at **2**. It rises when the PC is loud, caught,
or flagrantly abnormal; it falls with lying low or Visible Normalcy. Threshold
events at 4 / 6 / 8 / 10 are in the campaign guide. It is the GM's pacing dial —
turn it with a smile.

## 6. Getting stronger — Gear & Glow

The full equipment compendium (garage weapons, Wendell's gadgets, Glorptech
artifacts with Quirks), the **Glow** advancement system (1 per act: +1 stat,
new power, or a SUBLIME unlock), the three power tiers
(✨ Spark / ⚡ Surge / 🌟 SUBLIME), and the **Marks** table for hitting 0 HP all
live in **`gear-and-glow.md`** — and in the app's Sheet tab, which tracks all
of it with cards and pickers.
