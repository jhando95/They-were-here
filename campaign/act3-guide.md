# ACT THREE — Founders' Day
### Scene-by-scene GM guide · ~120 minutes · ⚠️ GM EYES ONLY

**The act in one sentence:** a three-target heist under fireworks, the boss
politely unzips, and Pinebrook is saved by salt, love, and aggressive human
weirdness.

**Structure:** Plan → Salt Run → the Three Sabotages (intercut) → GLORP PRIME →
Wake the Neighbors → Epilogues. 🎬 Open with **Act Three: Founders' Day**.

---

## Scene 1 — The Plan

📋 **The Plan** active. Lay the three targets on the table (map open, GM Screen
on, drop tokens as they talk):

| Target | Where | What success does |
|---|---|---|
| **A. The fireworks cache** | Community Center loading dock | No spore-seeding: the Bloom fizzles even if the beacon fires |
| **B. The beacon dish** | Top of the water tower | No ALL IS READY signal: the fleet holds at the moon |
| **C. The pod vault** | The Undermart | 47 pajama'd neighbors wake up — witnesses, chaos, and Gary |

**The rule that makes the finale work:** the PC personally runs ONE target.
Each other target gets an ally team and resolves on **one ally roll (d20+4)**
at the dramatic moment — the PC can spend Luck on those rolls (that's what
Luck was for all along). Failures don't kill targets; they add complications
to the boss fight (below).

Natural assignments (any mix works):
- Deborah + Form 77-B requisitions the loading dock. Legally. Terrifyingly.
- Priya + Petunia + a payload of pool salt for the dish. Wendell's jammer as backup.
- The PC + the Overseer's Glove problem: the vault needs "the handshake" —
  Crisp's glove (in his golf cart during the speech — Brains DC 12 lift) or
  his actual hand (that's a boss-fight objective; also fine).

## Scene 2 — The Salt Run (15 min, pure comedy with a timer)

Pinebrook's arsenal, sourced in one montage afternoon:
- **FreshMart aisle 7** — the coupon from the block party works. The clone
  cashier double-bags. "HYDRATION IS IMPORTANT, FELLOW HUMAN."
- **The pool supply store** — 25 lb bags. The owner asks zero questions. He's
  been waiting for this since '87. He salutes.
- **Duke's** — Marla empties the margarita station. Rimmers for everyone
  (compendium: Margarita Rimmer ×3).

Give everything a card in the app (compendium picker). If the player invents a
delivery system — brine in the sprinkler mains, the T-ball machine loaded with
salt shakers, Gnorman hollowed out and packed like a piñata — **it works**, and
it earns a Luck token. This scene exists to load Chekhov's entire garage.

## Scene 3 — Founders' Day: The Three Sabotages (intercut)

📋 **Sabotage at Sundown** active. Run it like TV: cut between targets at
cliffhangers. The town: bunting, folding chairs, marching band tuning,
"ONE. TWO. ONE. TWO." into the mic, forever. 300 lawns all leaning, faintly,
toward the tower.

**A. Loading dock.** Deborah presents Form 77-B to two Neighborly Units.
Ally roll: success = the crates are hers ("SIGNATURE VALID. COMPLIANCE...
DELICIOUS."); failure = they comply *and* alert Crisp (boss knows the plan;
GLORP PRIME opens with Spore Burst). Either way: the fireworks get salted or
swapped for Wendell's "normal, legal fireworks" (they spell WOW, sideways).

**B. The dish.** Priya's drone climbs with the payload; the tower's gnome ring
rotates to track it. Ally roll: success = salt across the emitter, the dish
sings one sour note and dies; failure = Petunia is pecked down by a Flamingo
vanguard — the beacon survives, and the boss fight gains a live countdown
(see below). PC on this target instead: Brawn DC 12 climb + Brains DC 12 rig,
guarded by 2 Flamingo Sentinels — staring contests on a ladder.

**C. The vault.** With the Glove: pods open on a running tap of d8 "What's in
This Pod" rolls — 47 neighbors in pajamas surface-stream into the square mid-
speech, asking who's been watering their plastic flowers. If the PC is here,
give them Gary's pod last. He wakes holding the gnome: "...did I miss the
block party?" Sheriff Marsh wakes *ready*, badge already out.

**Suspicion payoff:** at Watch 8+, the wellness-check pair shadows the PC all
day — a recurring two-clone tail that must be ditched (any stat DC 12, new
method each time; repeats auto-fail and cost 1 round in the boss fight).

**MARSH-2 runs Founders' Day "security."** She works the crowd all day —
her "I'M CONCERNED" aura makes every public move harder, and if Reputation is
7+, she has the Committal van parked behind the bandshell *for the PC*. Two
ways to neutralize her before the boss: the coffee demonstration in front of
the crowd (order her old usual, film the buffer — the town gasps), or
Deborah's Section 9 procedural freeze. Done publicly, either one is worth
−2 Reputation and turns the crowd from jury into audience. If the real Marsh
is freed from her pod mid-fight, she walks up to her double, badge out:
"You forgot the handshake." Let the table cheer.

## Scene 4 — GLORP PRIME

Trigger: any two targets resolve, or the PC confronts Crisp. The mic feedback
dies. 🎬 Play **Mr. Crisp Would Like a Word**. Then he unzips. Politely.
GLORP PRIME absorbs the potluck table first — *always* the potluck table
first, Mrs. Pemberton's casserole audibly delighted to be included.

**Base stats (npcs-and-monsters.md):** HP 30 · Dodge 10 · 2 actions/round
(Turf Slam +4/1d8 · Spore Burst Weird DC 12 · Absorb +1d6 HP, funnier each time).

**Adjust by sabotage outcomes:**
- Lawn Graffiti side quest done → start at **HP 25**
- Fireworks salted → Spore Burst deals no damage, only hiccups
- Beacon still live → **countdown: 5 rounds** to ALL IS READY; reaching the
  tower mid-fight (full move + Brawn DC 12) with salt kills the signal
- Pods open → each round, d6: on 5–6 a pajama'd neighbor does something
  useful (Marsh tackles, the real mailman delivers a 25 lb "package" of pool
  salt to the PC, priority overnight)

**The Weirdness Gambit — the finale mechanic, run it hot:**
each round, every act of *genuine human weirdness* on the field removes one of
GLORP PRIME's actions (max 2 — a fully buffered round shows him rendering an
hourglass). Count, loudly, like a sports commentator:
- an ally doing their signature bit (each ally = 1 charge, once each)
- the PC using a power flamboyantly (Sublime = automatic weirdness)
- callbacks to the Session Zero answers (each = weirdness + 1 free Luck)
- the marching band breaking into free jazz (Charm DC 12 to incite; the tuba
  player has been WAITING)
- Biscuit, sunglasses. It works exactly once. It is worth it.

**Salt:** double damage, as always. A margarita-rimmed bat is 2d6; the pool
salt burst is 2d6 adjacent; the sprinkler-brine Sublime is the tactical nuke.

**Crisp's dialogue while fighting** (he never stops being polite):
"I AM RECEIVING YOUR FEEDBACK." · "THIS IS A FUN GAME, FELLOW BEINGS." ·
(at half HP, quieter) "...we watched your broadcasts for forty years. we only
wanted to live in one."

That last line is the finale's hinge. If the player talks instead of swinging —
let them. Charm DC 16, dropping to DC 8 if they invoke their Session Zero
answers ("what do you love about your dumb little town") to explain what the
broadcasts *got wrong*. Success = the Treaty path opens mid-fight.

## Scene 5 — Wake the Neighbors & Endings

📋 **Wake the Neighbors** as the dust settles. Then pick the ending the player
earned (they'll know which one it is):

- **🕊 The Treaty of Pinebrook** — 🎬 play it. The Collective is mortified,
  releases everyone, re-sods the lawns properly, keeps one model home as an
  embassy. The gift baskets begin immediately.
- **💥 The Big Boom** — 🎬 play it. The tower goes up; the fleet reroutes to
  Ohio. Salt rain. Gary hugs the PC and never lets go of the gnome.
- **🌱 Pinebrook, Perfected** — 🎬 only if the player truly lost (both
  fireworks AND beacon failed, boss unbeaten). Play it straight, no jokes,
  then offer the sequel hook: *someone* is still human out there. Season two.

**Epilogues:** roll or choose from the d6 table in `tables.md` for each
surviving ally, but hand-write two:
- **Biscuit** — gets the whole steak. Every year, on the anniversary, another
  steak appears on the porch. Nobody sees who leaves it. Biscuit knows.
- **The PC** — return to their Session Zero answers one last time: "What does
  everyone in Pinebrook know you for?" Ask it again. Let them answer as the
  person they are *now*. Roll credits. 🎬 (If you want a stinger: one gnome,
  somewhere, rotates three degrees. Cut to black.)

**Final bookkeeping:** award 1 Glow (for the road — sequels exist), export the
save from the app, and label it something like `pinebrook-final.json`. That
file is the campaign's trophy.

## Act Three troubleshooting

| Situation | Fix |
|---|---|
| The plan is genuinely better than yours | It works. Never punish better writing. Move the drama to execution costs. |
| PC goes down mid-boss | Marks table (0 HP is never death) — and the allies get one full round of Weirdness while GLORP PRIME "PAUSES OUT OF RESPECT." Wake the PC at 1 HP with Diane pepper-spraying the boss, screaming an apology. |
| Player wants to join the Collective | Incredible. Run the negotiation — the Collective would LOVE a human liaison. Ending: Pinebrook gets an ambassador, the PC gets a sash, Biscuit gets dual citizenship. Valid win. |
| Nobody thinks to use salt | Marla, mid-fight, margarita rimmer in hand: "You gonna season that thing or WHAT?" |
