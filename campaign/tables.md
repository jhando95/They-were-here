2. Your phone autoplays the 4,000 lawn photos at max brightness.
3. Biscuit announces your location. Lovingly. Loudly.
4. You step on spore-grass; your shoe starts photosynthesizing.
5. A clone appears to offer help. Genuinely. It's worse.
6. Deborah Vance issues *you* a violation notice.
7. Your power misfires adorably (GM's choice).
8. Mrs. Pemberton arrives with a casserole. It is moving.
9. Car alarm. Every car. Except yours, which unlocks itself.
10. It is suddenly 2:47 AM. How is it 2:47 AM?
