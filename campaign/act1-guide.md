# ACT ONE — The Night It Happened
### Scene-by-scene GM guide · ~90–120 minutes · ⚠️ GM EYES ONLY

**The act in one sentence:** the player witnesses the impossible, discovers
nobody will believe them, and assembles the first proof — ending with the
whole town summoned to MANDATORY FUN.

**Act goals (any 3 of 4 = ready for Act Two):**
- ☐ Player has 3+ clues pinned to the Truth Board
- ☐ Player has met Biscuit, Marla, or Wendell
- ☐ Player knows the hum comes from the water tower
- ☐ The invitation has arrived

**App cues legend:** 🎬 = play a scene card · 📋 = flip a quest · 📌 = reveal a clue · 👁 = Suspicion

---

## Scene 0 — Session Zero (10 min, do not skip)

Run character creation from `rules.md` (or load a pregen in the app). Then ask
the three questions, and *write the answers down* — the finale is built from them:

1. *What does everyone in Pinebrook know you for?*
2. *What do you love most about your dumb little town?*
3. *Why were you awake at 2:47 AM?*

Whatever they answer to #3, nod slowly and say "...yeah, that's what you tell
people." Do not explain. 🎬 Play **They Were Here** (cold open) as you start.

**GM secret to establish silently:** the PC already Glows a little. Their power
(rolled at creation) came from *somewhere* — a childhood incident, a slushie in
'09, a dream about a very polite light. Ask them to pick which. Wrong answers welcome.

## Scene 1 — 2:47 AM (the abduction)

🎬 Play **Act One: The Night It Happened**, then run an ordinary late-night beat
first — let them microwave something, watch an infomercial, almost go back to
bed. The scare lands harder from inside boredom. Then: 🎬 **The Light Over
Marigold Lane**.

> The hum comes up through the floor. The light through the blinds is the
> wrong color for anything that lives on your street. And over the Hendersons'
> lawn, Gary Henderson is rising — bathrobe, garden gnome, an expression of
> mild inconvenience — into a cone of cyan light.

**Let the player try things. Nothing works, everything matters:**
- **Photograph/film it** — the phone stutters and saves **4,000 photos of a
  smiling lawn** (📌 *4,000 Lawn Photos*). Timestamp: 2:47. The lawn in the
  photos has stripes Gary's lawn won't have until tomorrow.
- **Run outside** (Brawn DC 8 to get there before it ends) — the light snaps
  off *the instant* their foot hits the porch. The hum recedes northeast — toward
  the water tower. The grass is warm and smells like a new shower curtain.
- **Shout / wake the street** — one porch light comes on: Mrs. Pemberton, who
  says "Gary always did overwater" and goes back inside. 👁 +1.
- **Do nothing and watch** — reward the horror-movie discipline: they see a
  second, smaller shape float up after Gary. (It's the gnome's twin. Do not explain.)

**End the scene** the moment the light dies. Hard cut: "It's 7 AM. Sprinklers."

## Scene 2 — The Morning After

> Gary Henderson is mowing his lawn. He has been mowing the same strip for
> three hours. He is wearing a suit. He sees you, raises one hand like he's
> practiced it in a mirror, and says: "HELLO, FELLOW HUMAN NEIGHBOR. IT SURE
> IS WEATHER TODAY."

📋 Activate **Something's Off About Gary**. GARY-2 is *friendly, wrong, and
unfailable* — he cannot be caught in a lie because he doesn't know he's lying.
Sprinkle tells from the d12 table (two per conversation, max). If the player
pushes hard with contradictions, he **buffers**: a 3-second pause, a too-wide
smile, "PLEASE HOLD." That's their first mechanical lesson about clones.

**Diane Henderson** (real, terrified): insists Gary is "just tired." If treated
kindly: "He called me 'FEMALE SPOUSE' this morning. As a joke. Gary doesn't
joke before coffee." She won't leave him. She will matter in Act Three.

**Reporting it:** Sheriff Marsh listens, takes real notes, and says the worst
possible thing: "I believe *you* believe that." Deputy Hodge takes a statement
in crayon and gets the gnome's hat color right *without being told*. (Plant
that now; pay it off whenever.)

**Pacing valve:** if the player stalls here, Biscuit starts howling at Gary
from inside the house — segue to Scene 3.

## Scene 3 — Breaking Into the Henderson House

The first real challenge. Entry options and DCs:
- Trellis to the upstairs window — Brawn DC 8 (DC 12 carrying anything dumb)
- Dog door — Brawn DC 12, dignity non-negotiable
- Spare key under the obviously fake rock — Brains DC 8 to spot
- "Watering the plants while they're away" bluff on GARY-2 — Charm DC 12; he
  APPROVES OF PLANT MAINTENANCE and lets them in. 👁 +1, and it's worth it.

Inside, five discoveries (let them find 3+; Biscuit nose-points to the rest):
1. Fridge: jars labeled **HUMAN FOOD** — all mayonnaise (📌 *HUMAN FOOD Jars*)
2. Sock drawer: potting soil. Just... soil.
3. Roof (via trellis): perfect **scorch ring** around the chimney (📌 *The Scorch Ring*)
4. Kitchen drawer: **brine receipts**, 14 gallons, purchased 2:47 PM (📌 *Brine Receipts*)
5. The bathtub: **Biscuit**, shaking. If shown kindness, he speaks: *"Okay. Okay.
   You can hear me. Great. GREAT. Two things: that is NOT Gary, and I have not
   had dinner."* 📋 Activate **Good Boy, Best Intel** (side quest).

**If GARY-2 catches them:** he offers lemonade. It is warm pickle brine. He
watches them drink it. 👁 +2. He does not report them — *he files a compliment.*
("NEIGHBOR VISITED. NEIGHBORLINESS: EXEMPLARY.") The Vanguard's records will
show this later. Comedy now, chills in Act Two.

## Scene 4 — The Thread-Pulling Tour (any order, ~10 min each)

📋 Activate **Paper Trail** and **The Lawn Frequency** as they engage.

**Duke's Diner.** Marla refills coffee uninvited at dramatically perfect
moments. Roll d6 Rumors freely. If the player shares what they saw, she doesn't
laugh: "Ninety-nine. Over the reservoir. Nobody believed me either." A fork is
stuck to the back of her hand. She hasn't noticed. (Seed **Magnet Fingers**.)

**Wendell's Wires.** Wendell whisper-yells them into the back room, where a
wall of scanners has been recording the **Lawn Frequency** for years — a spike
every night at 2:47, bearing: the water tower (📌 *The 2:47 Hum*). He is
*ecstatic* someone finally saw something: "THIRTY YEARS. THIRTY YEARS I'VE
BEEN THE CRAZY ONE." Gives them the **Blacklight Flashlight** (add via
compendium) as a welcome-to-the-truth gift.

**The Gazette archive.** Brains DC 8 (DC 12 if they antagonize the filing
system, which is sentient in spirit). 1987 front page: *LIGHTS OVER RESERVOIR;
THREE MISSING, RETURNED 'POLITE'* (📌 *Gazette, 1987*). Photo caption names one
of the three: **a young Mr. Crisp.** Let the player find that themselves —
don't point at it.

**The water tower (exterior).** Fresh ladder scuffs. The grass around the legs
is too green, has no smell, and *leans toward them* (📌 *Spore-Grass Sample*
if they take a cutting — under blacklight it glows; salt makes it hiss). If
they climb: Brawn DC 12. From the top, the whole town looks... arranged. A
drone-pigeon watches them, one beat too long. (Seed **Birds Aren't Real**.)
Climbing at night: 👁 +2 and a gnome is on their lawn when they get home.

## Scene 5 — Act Break: MANDATORY FUN

Trigger this when the act goals are mostly checked, or the table's energy dips.
The PC's porch. Every porch. 🎬 Play **Mandatory Fun**. 📋 Activate **MANDATORY FUN**;
mark Act One's done quests; 📋 activate **Block Party Infiltration**.

> Heavy cardstock. Smells faintly of pickle brine.
> NEIGHBORHOOD IMPROVEMENT ASSOCIATION · BLOCK PARTY — SATURDAY ·
> ATTENDANCE WILL BE TAKEN · Games! Prizes! Jell-O!
> *Please verify you are having fun.*

**End-of-act checklist:**
- ☐ Award **1 Glow** (walk the player through spending it — the app's Sheet tab)
- ☐ Suspicion check: if 4+, the gnome event has fired — describe it fondly
- ☐ Ask: "What does your character tell themselves tonight to get to sleep?"
  Write the answer down. It's finale ammunition.

## Act One troubleshooting

| The player... | Do this |
|---|---|
| Tries to leave town for help | The county road has a DETOUR sign. All four of them. The detour is a perfect loop. Play it dry: Pinebrook is not sealed — the *road crew* is just "resurfacing." Forever. |
| Attacks GARY-2 immediately | He does not fight back. He bleeds green glitter, says "I FORGIVE YOU, FELLOW HUMAN," and waves to a neighbor. 👁 +3. The whole street saw. Now *the player* looks like the strange one — that's the real cost, teach it early. |
| Won't investigate, hides at home | 2:47 AM: the hum again — closer. Through the wall. Mrs. Abernathy next door is gone by morning and her replacement waters plastic flowers. The town comes to them. |
| Solves everything in 20 minutes | Beautiful — let Act One be short. Pinebrook rewards momentum with dread, not padding. |
