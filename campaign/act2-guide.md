# ACT TWO — Welcome to the Neighborhood
### Scene-by-scene GM guide · ~120 minutes · ⚠️ GM EYES ONLY

**The act in one sentence:** the player builds a crew of weirdos, infiltrates
the block party, loses the last adult in the room, and finds out where the
neighbors actually are.

**Act goals (need all 3 for Act Three):**
- ☐ 2+ allies recruited
- ☐ Crisp's keycard obtained
- ☐ The Undermart found — and the Founders' Day plan understood

**Tone shift:** Act One was "nobody believes me." Act Two is "some people do —
and that makes it real." Keep the jokes coming, but let silences land.

---

## Scene 1 — The Town Is Changing (montage)

🎬 Play **Act Two: Welcome to the Neighborhood**. Then run a quick montage —
three vignettes, player reacts to each, no rolls unless they engage:

1. The **Ruiz family** laughs at a joke two seconds late. In unison. In *harmony*.
2. The **mailman** has a new smile. Count the teeth with your eyes. Don't finish counting.
3. Every replaced house now has the **same gnome** (📌 *The New Gnomes*).
   Priya's older brother swears one filmed him doing kickflips. "It ADJUSTED
   ITS ANGLE, man."

**Rule reveal via play:** if the player destroys a gnome — it's cathartic,
d4 to smash — it *files a report as it dies* (a tiny voice: "VANDALISM.
NOTED. FORGIVEN.") 👁 +1.

## Scene 2 — Recruiting the Weirdos

Each ally has a *want*, a *scene*, and a *mechanical gift*. Run the ones the
player pursues; let the others join late (or in the finale, dramatically).
📋 Activate **Friends in Weird Places**.

**Wendell Diaz** — *wants to be believed.* Already in if Act One went well.
Gift: gadgets (Jammer Brick, EMP Doorbell via compendium) and the TOTALLY
FLORAL DELIVERY van. His van scene: he's stenciled the flowers himself.
They're carnations. "NOBODY SUSPECTS CARNATIONS."

**Priya Chandrasekhar** — *wants snacks and to be taken seriously, in that
order.* Scene: AV room, lunch, she's already tracking the gnome network on a
corkboard that would make the FBI weep. Hire rate: Emergency Snacks. Her drone
photos reveal the lawns form a pattern (📌 *The Antenna Pattern*). Gift:
"Petunia" the drone + aerial recon.

**Marla Bickle** — *wants her hands back.* 📋 **Magnet Fingers**: health
inspection Friday; every fork in Duke's is on her. Build the Grounding
Gauntlet with Wendell (Brains DC 12, or DC 8 with a good plan). Done: she
joins, her Magnet Touch turns *precise*, and at the block party her handshake
can lift Crisp's keycard clean. Also she brings pie. The pie is safe. Probably.

**Deborah Vance** — *wants the bylaws respected.* 📋 **Release the Deborah**:
show her evidence Crisp violated **Section 4: Unapproved Structures** (the
beacon dish counts, so does the antenna-lawn). Charm DC 16 — drops to DC 8
with documents in hand. Recruited, she is a force of nature: clones detect her
approach at 200 feet and begin *straightening*. Gift: Form 77-B.

**Biscuit** — *wants steak and Gary back*, and to be a good boy, which he is.
His nose knows the FreshMart basement "smells like the bad math feeling."
Non-negotiable canon: Biscuit survives the campaign.

## Scene 3 — Mid-act gut punch: THERE IS NO CRIME IN PINEBROOK

Place this **between** recruitment scenes, unannounced:

> Sheriff Marsh calls you. Actually calls you. "Listen. I pulled the '87 file
> after you left. I need you to come in tomorrow, and I need you to bring —"
> The line goes quiet for three seconds. Then her voice again, warmer,
> smoother: "THERE IS NO CRIME IN PINEBROOK, FELLOW CITIZEN. DISREGARD.
> IT SURE IS WEATHER TODAY."

MARSH-2 by morning. Fresh gnome outside the station. Deputy Hodge, spared —
"template incoherent" — keeps crayon-drawing what he saw that night: a light,
a ladder, a hat. He gives the drawings to the PC. "I drew you too. You're the
one with the good posture."

👁 +1 automatically. Authority is gone; it's the weirdos or nobody now.

## Scene 4 — Set Piece: The Block Party

The Community Center lot, Saturday. 🎬 The invitation already played; open on
sensory wrongness: bunting perfectly level, folding chairs plotted by laser,
Jell-O in shapes Jell-O doesn't hold.

Run it as **three stations + one objective**. Each station is a soft humanity
test; each pass/fail nudges the scene.

1. **"ARE YOU EVEN HUMAN?" trivia.** Sample questions, deliver deadpan:
   "What is the boiling point of nostalgia?" · "How many is a casserole for?"
   · "Complete the phrase: HELLO, FELLOW ______." Wrong answers are safe;
   *confident* answers get flagged (👁 +1). The correct human answer is a
   shrug — literally teach the player that shrugging reads as human.
2. **The Jell-O station.** Casserole Sense or Brains DC 12: one tray wobbles
   *against* the breeze (📌 confirm spore Jell-O). Eating it anyway: Weird DC 12
   or hiccup glowing bubbles for the rest of the party (👁 +2, deeply funny).
3. **The three-legged race.** Clones are eerily good; their legs *merge*.
   Winning anyway (Brawn DC 16, or DC 12 with an ally partner): the crowd's
   applause is one clap, all together, once. Prize: a coupon for FreshMart
   aisle 7. (They're *giving the humans salt.* They think the urns are decorative.
   Let a sharp player clock the dramatic irony.)

**The objective — Crisp's keycard** (📋 **Block Party Infiltration**):
- Marla's handshake (auto with Grounding Gauntlet; Charm DC 8 to set up), or
- Pickpocket during his speech (Brains DC 12; the speech is 40 minutes of mic
  testing: "ONE. TWO. ONE. TWO."), or
- Any plan involving the potluck table, which the GM is honor-bound to allow.

**Crisp up close:** never sweats. Drinks from a mayonnaise jar with a straw
(📌 *The Mayo Thing*). If spoken to, he's *warm*, in the way a laminated card
is warm from the copier: "I HAVE HEARD EXCELLENT COMPLIMENTS ABOUT YOUR
NEIGHBORLINESS." (That's GARY-2's filed compliment. Let the player's blood run cold.)

**If it goes loud:** the **Flamingo Sentinels** activate — but only move when
unobserved. Run the fight as a lights-flicker staring contest with d12 pecks in
the dark. Clones do not brawl at parties; they *escort*, politely, toward the
punch table. 👁 +2 for a loud exit.

## Scene 5 — The Undermart

Keycard + freight elevator behind the yogurt (Biscuit or the blacklight finds
it; Brains DC 8 once they're looking). The elevator has one button. It is
labeled **DOWN, PLEASE**.

🎬 Play **Comfort: Maximum** the moment the doors open. 👁 Reveal the
**Undermart** location on the map. 📋 **The FreshMart Basement** progresses.

Give them time in the pod hall. No enemies yet. Let them find Gary (gnome and
all), Sheriff Marsh, the real mailman (normal number of teeth). 📌 *The Pod
Manifest*. Pods won't open: each has a hand-shaped recess. Glorptech. **The
Overseer's handshake** (📋 activate **The Beacon** as the schematic wall
explains Founders' Day — 📌 *The Fireworks Invoice*).

**Then the escape chase.** THE MAILMAN steps out of the dark sorting alcove:
"YOU ARE OUTSIDE YOUR DELIVERY AREA." Miniboss + 2 Gnome Drones, run as a
chase through the stockroom: three obstacles (yogurt avalanche — Brawn DC 8;
box-crusher conveyor — Brains DC 12; the LOADING DOCK, PLEASE door — Charm
DC 8 to talk the door's courtesy protocol into opening). Standing and fighting
is legal but loud: 👁 +2 and the whole Vanguard learns their face.

**Loot the escape:** grab-what-you-can as they run — offer one **Glorptech**
item from the compendium (Glorp Goo Jar or Spore Bloom in a Jar are ideal;
roll 🎁 Loot if they'd rather gamble). Quirks apply immediately and forever.

## Scene 6 — Act Break: The Week Before Founders' Day

Safehouse scene (Wendell's back room, everyone around the corkboard).
📋 Mark Act Two quests done; 📋 activate **The Plan**. Let the player lay out
what they know — correct gently via allies. End on Deborah, of all people,
quietly: "My husband was replaced in *March*. I filed a complaint." Beat.
"They apologized in *writing*."

**End-of-act checklist:**
- ☐ Award **1 Glow**
- ☐ Every recruited ally = one **Weirdness charge** banked for the finale
- ☐ Suspicion 6+? The gnomes now track the PC's car like sunflowers track the sun
- ☐ Ask: "Who are you most afraid you can't save?" Write it down.

## Act Two troubleshooting

| Situation | Fix |
|---|---|
| Player skips the block party | Attendance was mandatory: a WELLNESS CHECK visits at dawn (two Neighborly Units, a clipboard, a bundt cake). 👁 +2 and the keycard now requires the Scene 5 heist to go in blind — harder, still fair. |
| Player frees a pod early | They can't (the handshake), but a good plan cracks ONE pod's chiller: the guest half-wakes and murmurs "five more minutes." Gut punch, no jailbreak. |
| Too many allies, scenes drag | Allies self-organize off-screen ("Priya's teaching Deborah to fly Petunia; we don't discuss how that's going"). Keep the PC's spotlight. |
| Player wants to confront Crisp NOW | Let them! He receives accusations like customer feedback: "THANK YOU. YOUR CONCERN HAS BEEN LOGGED AS ENTHUSIASM." Nothing breaks him before the finale — but the attempt earns a Luck token and terrifies the allies. |
