# Gear & Glow
### The equipment, artifact, and advancement chapter of the PORCHLIGHT system

This is the campaign's "magic items and spellbook" chapter. Everything here is
also built into the app: the Sheet tab has an item compendium picker, power
cards with tier tracking, and a Glow counter.

---

## 1. Gear

Three tiers of stuff:

| Tier | What it is | How you get it |
|---|---|---|
| ⚪ **Mundane** | Garage, kitchen, and FreshMart aisle 7 | Just take it (or roll Garage Treasures) |
| 🔧 **Modified** | Wendell or Priya built it, probably at 3 AM | Quest rewards, favors, side quests |
| 🟢 **Glorptech** | Alien salvage. Powerful. Every piece has a **Quirk** you don't get to refuse | Stolen, salvaged, gifted by a mortified Collective |

**Carrying stuff:** no encumbrance math. You can carry what fits in two hands, a
fanny pack, and your dignity. The GM may rule that a 25 lb bag of pool salt and
a T-ball pitching machine is a *choice*.

**Salt** is the campaign's silver bullet: anything that counts as salt deals
**double damage** to gelatinous beings and dissolves spore-grass on contact.

## 2. The Glow (advancement)

Alien exposure accumulates. In this campaign, you don't level up — you **Glow up**.

**You gain 1 Glow at the end of each act** (the GM may award a bonus Glow for a
completed side-quest chain or an all-timer session). Spend a Glow, at any rest,
on ONE of:

1. **+1 to one stat** (max +4)
2. **A new Wacky Power** — roll on the d20 table twice, keep one
3. **Unlock a power's SUBLIME tier** (see below)

The app's Sheet tab tracks Glow and power tiers.

## 3. Power tiers (the "spellbook")

Every power has three tiers. Everyone starts with Spark + Surge.

- ✨ **Spark** — free, minor, flavorful. Use constantly.
- ⚡ **Surge** — costs 1 WP. The scene-changer (this is the power as written).
- 🌟 **SUBLIME** — requires a Glow to unlock. Costs 2 WP, once per session.
  Big, loud, and canon-warping.

## 4. Marks (what 0 HP costs you)

You don't die in Pinebrook — the Collective finds violence *gauche* — but going
down leaves a Mark. Roll d6 the first time each session you hit 0 HP:

1. **The Bandage.** Worn like a medal. Pure style. Start next session with +1 Luck.
2. **Glorp-Stained Arm.** Glows faintly near alien activity — free detection, but you cannot hide in the dark.
3. **The Tic.** You count stairs out loud now. In a whisper. You've noticed. So have they.
4. **Biscuit's Ward.** Biscuit refuses to leave your side. Ally always present; snack budget doubled.
5. **The Clock.** You wake at 2:47 AM forever (gain the Night Owl Clock spark, keep your own power).
6. **The Gnome.** Weird +1, permanently — and a gnome now stands on your lawn. You cannot prove it moved. It moved.
